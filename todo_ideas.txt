Don't stop at the first already-seen item
One listing might have been sold and a new one appeared in its slot. If you stop at the first match, you'll miss genuinely new listings further down.

Better rule: stop after N consecutive already-seen items (pick 3 or 5). If you see 3 in a row that you already have, the rest of the page is almost certainly old too. Reset the counter whenever you hit a new one.
